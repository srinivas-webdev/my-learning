        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          In <span class="dark:text-red-300 font-bold ">casual conversation</span>, 
          **adjective forms** are sometimes used to **modify verbs**.  
          Speakers may omit the **-ly** that would make an adjective into an adverb.  
          In addition, the adjective 
          <mark class="bg-pink-300 italic font-bold">good</mark> 
          is used by some people in place of the adverb <mark class="bg-pink-300 italic font-bold">well</mark>:
          <ul>
            <li>
              <div class="grid grid-cols-[1fr_1fr] gap-1 sm:w-4/5">
                <span>You could do this <mark class="italic font-bold">quick</mark>.</span>
                <span>[instead of quickly]</span>
              </div>
            </li>
            <li>
              <div class="grid grid-cols-[1fr_1fr] gap-1 sm:w-4/5">
                <span>Look at how <mark class="italic font-bold">slow</mark> this car is going.</span>
                <span>[instead of slowly]</span>
              </div>
            </li>
            <li>
              <div class="grid grid-cols-[1fr_1fr] gap-1 sm:w-4/5">
                <span>I know you'll do <mark class="italic font-bold">good</mark> on the test.</span>
                <span>[instead of well]</span>
              </div>
            </li>
          </ul>   
        </div>
      </li>
      <li class="flex flex-col">
          <div class="flex ">
            <svg class="w-8 h-8 shrink-0 text-fg-brand me-1.5" aria-hidden="true" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" fill="none" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke="currentColor" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M8.5 11.5 11 14l4-4m6 2a9 9 0 1 1-18 0 9 9 0 0 1 18 0Z"/></svg>
            <span class="text-body font-bold">
              <mark class="bg-[#ffff00] px-1">Be careful!</mark> 
              In <mark class="bg-purple-300">more formal speech</mark>
              and especially in 
              <mark class="bg-purple-300">informational writing</mark> 
              (newspaper and academic writing), adjective forms are almost never used to modify verbs.   
              Instead, <mark class="bg-green-200">adverbs</mark> are used:
            </span>
          </div>
          - When air is inhaled into the lungs, evaporation occurs <mark class="bg-green-200 font-bold italic">quickly</mark>. (ACAD.)
          - The reactions would proceed very <mark class="bg-green-200 font-bold italic">slowly</mark>. (ACAD.)
          - Financial services stocks also did <mark class="bg-green-200 font-bold italic">well</mark>, gaining 55.4%. (NEWS)
        </li>
    </ol>
