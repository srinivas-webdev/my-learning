          <mark class="bg-green-300 font-bold ">Linking verbs</mark>

        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          The linking verb <mark class="bg-green-300 font-bold ">be</mark> 
          is common in both conversation and writing.  
          But otherwise, some linking verbs are preferred in conversation; others in writing.  
          
          Some of the most common linking verbs are **verbs of the senses**.   
          They include <mark class="font-bold">“taste,” “smell,” “feel,” “look,”</mark> and <mark class="font-bold">“sound.”</mark>    
          These verbs often **link** an **adjective** to a **subject**.   

          Here is a list of the **most common linking verbs** used either in 
          <mark class="font-bold ">conversation</mark> or in
          <mark class="font-bold ">writing</mark>:
