        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          are verbs that are **completed by a subject complement** – 
          <mark class="font-bold">the adjective or noun phrase</mark> that describes the subject.  
          Many grammar experts consider the verb **BE** to be a linking verb, 
          but others say BE verb patterns are sometimes different from other linking verb patterns.   
          This is because **adverbial information often follows <mark>BE</mark> verbs**, such as in the following sentence:
          - Bob is at the school.
       </div>
