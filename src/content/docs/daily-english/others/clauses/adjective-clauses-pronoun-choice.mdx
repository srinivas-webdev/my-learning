          <mark class="font-bold">Adjective Clauses</mark> (also called "relative clauses") are used to
          <mark class="font-bold">modify a noun</mark>.   
          An adjective clause usually <mark class="bg-sky-300 font-bold">begins with a relative pronoun</mark> 
          (who, which, that, etc.).   
          There are several options for 
          <mark class="bg-sky-300 font-bold">relative pronoun choice</mark> in an adjective clause:
          - **who** - (people)
          - **which** - (things)
          - **that** - (people and things)
        </div>
