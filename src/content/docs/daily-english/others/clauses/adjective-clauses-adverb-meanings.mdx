        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          <mark class="font-bold">Adjective Clauses</mark> can be used to modify nouns that refer to **time** or **place**.  
          <mark class="font-bold">Adjective clauses about place</mark> usually begin with 
          **where**, **in which**, or **that**. 
          - That's the **house where** I live. 
          - That's the **house in which** I live. 
          - That's the **house that** I live in.

          <mark class="font-bold">Adjective clauses about time</mark> can use 
          **when**, **that**, or **no relative pronoun**.
          - I remember the **day when** he left.  
          - I remember the **day that** he left.  
          - I remember the **day** he left.
        </div>
