---
title: Did you want more coffee?
description: Simple Past Tense in Polite Offers
---

In **<span style="color:orangered;">conversation</span>**, sometimes people use the **simple past** of **want** or **need** in order to 
**make an offer** or **ask someone's preference**.   
Questions are formed with **<span style="color:orangered;">_did + want/need_</span>**, but they refer to the **present time**:   

- **_Did_** you **_want_** more coffee? [Waiter asking a customer.]
- **_Did_** you **_need_** your receipt? [Clerk asking a customer.]
