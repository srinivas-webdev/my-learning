          <span class="text-body font-bold">What does the Research show?</span>
        </div> 
        <div class="ml-10 bg-gradient-to-r from-lime-200 to-lime-300 text-gray-950 p-1 rounded-md shadow-md">
          In <span class="text-rose-600 font-bold">conversation</span>, sometimes people use the **simple past** of **want** or **need** in order to 
          **make an offer** or **ask someone's preference**.   
          Questions are formed with <span class="text-rose-600 font-bold">_did + want/need_</span>, but they refer to the **present time**:   

          - **_Did_** you **_want_** more coffee? [Waiter asking a customer.]
          - **_Did_** you **_need_** your receipt? [Clerk asking a customer.]
        </div>
      </li>
      <li class="flex flex-col">
        <div class="flex ">
          <svg class="w-8 h-8 shrink-0 text-fg-brand me-1.5" aria-hidden="true" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" fill="none" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke="currentColor" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M8.5 11.5 11 14l4-4m6 2a9 9 0 1 1-18 0 9 9 0 0 1 18 0Z"/></svg>
          <span class="text-body font-bold">
            Answers to these questions are usually <mark class="bg-sky-300">NOT in the simple past</mark>. 
            They can be in the <mark class="bg-pink-300">simple present</mark>,
            <mark class="bg-pink-300">present progressive</mark>, 
            or <mark class="bg-pink-300">future</mark>, 
            or a simple answer may have <mark class="bg-pink-300">no verb</mark>:
          </span>
        </div>
        <div class="ml-10 flex flex-col divide-y-2 divide-solid divide-indigo-200 shadow-lg">
          <div class="grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] bg-violet-200 dark:bg-purple-900 rounded-t-lg font-bold justify-items-center">
            <span class="grid w-full place-items-center border-r-2 border-r-orange-500">Tense of Answer</span>
            <span class="grid w-full place-items-center">Example</span>
          </div>
          <div class="grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-purple-800 font-bold mt-0">
            <span class="grid w-full border-r-2 border-orange-500 p-2">1. Simple Present</span>
            <span class="flex flex-col p-2 gap-2">
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">WAITER:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight"><mark>Did</mark> you <mark>need</mark> milk or cream for your coffee?</span>
              </span>
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">CUSTOMER:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight">No, I'm fine.*</span>
              </span>
            </span>
          </div>
          <div class="grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-purple-800 font-bold mt-0">
            <span class="grid w-full border-r-2 border-orange-500 p-2">2. Present progressive </span>
            <span class="flex flex-col p-2 gap-2">
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Jane:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight"><mark>Did</mark> you <mark>want</mark> to go to a movie?</span>
              </span>
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Kate:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight">Uh-uh. I'<mark>m reading</mark> a book.</span>
              </span>
            </span>
          </div>
          <div class="grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-purple-800 font-bold mt-0">
            <span class="grid w-full border-r-2 border-orange-500 p-2">3. Future </span>
            <span class="flex flex-col p-2 gap-2">
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Bob:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight"><mark>Did</mark> you <mark>want</mark> to try one of these drinks?</span>
              </span>
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Tom:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight">Yeah, I'<mark>ll have</mark> one.</span>
              </span>
            </span>
          </div>
          <div class="grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-purple-800 font-bold mt-0">
            <span class="grid w-full border-r-2 border-orange-500 p-2">4. no verb </span>
            <span class="flex flex-col p-2 gap-2">
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Kate:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight"><mark>Did</mark> you <mark>want</mark> chocolate on top of those?</span>
              </span>
              <span class="flex flex-col sm:grid sm:grid-cols-[1fr_3fr] gap-0 sm:gap-2">
                <span class="leading-tight sm:justify-self-end">Bob:</span>
                <span class="leading-tight"><mark>No thanks.</mark></span>
              </span>
            </span>
          </div>
        </div>
        
        <div class="ml-10 font-bold">
          <sup class="">*</sup> When saying "No" to an offer, Americans often say, 
          "I'm fine," meaning "I'm fine without that."
        </div>
      </li>
