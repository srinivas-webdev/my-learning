          <span class="text-body font-bold">Reporting what someone said</span>
        </div> 
        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          There are two ways to report what someone said: 
          - **Direct speech**
            - quotes the exact words of the peaker, often introducing them with the "quoting verbs" said or asked.
          - **Indirect speech**
            - does not use the exact words of the speaker. It uses a reporting verb and an indirect statement (or "noun clause").
        </div>

        <div class="ml-10 leading-tight">
          In real conversations,  **direct speech**, is <mark class="bg-lime-200 font-bold">rarely an exact quote</mark>
          of of previous speech.  
          The direct speech may **reword the idea**, **provide a summary**, or even **express the speaker's thoughts** more than exact speech.
